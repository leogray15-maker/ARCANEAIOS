1/ You are being held in place by exactly one thing. Not five. One.

2/ It is called a constraint. A bottleneck. Like a PC where the GPU is fine and the CPU is choking it. You can upgrade everything else and the frame rate does not move.

3/ There are only three possible constraints. Skills. Traits. Beliefs. Right now one of them is yours.

4/ Skills is the one everyone thinks it is. It is what the side-hustle videos sell. It is almost never it. You already know how to do the work. Be honest.

5/ Traits is the one you avoid. Impulse control. Persistence. What you do when it is boring. If work still feels like work, this is you. And no course sells a trait.

6/ Beliefs is the quiet one. You are surrounded by average, and you are absorbing it every day you are not actively topping up. Use it or lose it applies to the mind.

7/ Traits are fixed by acting like the person who already has them, long enough that it burns in. Beliefs are fixed by what you listen to every morning. Neither is a skill.

8/ Find your one. Stop upgrading the parts that were never the problem. The full breakdown is in the Archives.
