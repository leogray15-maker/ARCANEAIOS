It is not a skill you are missing.

You already know how to do the thing. You have read the course. You have watched the breakdown. You could explain the business model to a stranger.

And you are still where you were.

There are three constraints. Skills. Traits. Beliefs.

Skills is the one that gets views. Traits and beliefs are the ones that get you paid.

Stop buying the skill. Look at what you do when the work feels like work.
