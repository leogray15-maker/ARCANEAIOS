Subject: Which one is it
Preview: You already know how to do the work. So what is actually stopping you.

Quick one.

You have read the model. You know the steps. If I asked you to explain it to someone you could do it without notes.

And you are still where you were three months ago.

That is not a skills problem. I know it feels like one, because that is what every video sells. Learn this, then you are free. You learned it. You are not free.

There are three constraints. Skills. Traits. Beliefs. One of them is yours.

Skills is the clickbait one. Almost never the real one.

Traits is the one nobody wants to look at. Impulse control. What your character does when the work is boring and the result is far away. If setbacks still land like pain, this is you. And you fix it by acting like the person who already has the trait, every day, until it burns in. That is the whole method. Remind yourself who you are every morning, like you have no memory.

Beliefs is the one holding most people. You are surrounded by average and you are soaking it up, quietly, every day you are not putting something better in. A mind is a muscle. Stop training it and it shrinks.

So. Which one is it. You know.
