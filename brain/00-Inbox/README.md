# Inbox

Drop anything here. Ideas, pastes, half-thoughts, screenshots.

SPARK owns this folder: on a sweep, every idea gets a verdict — BUILD, WATCH
or KILL — and either becomes an order in `06-Orders/Orders.md`, a decision in
`04-Records/Decisions/`, or moves to `Killed` with one line on why.

Nothing stays here longer than a week.
